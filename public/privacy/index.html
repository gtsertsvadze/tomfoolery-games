Privacy.

Last updated 27 September 2026

tomfoolery.games is a handful of tiny games. There are no accounts, no ads and no cookies, and nothing about you is sold or shared.

What gets stored

Games only keep anonymous tallies, like how many people gave each answer overall and today. Nothing links an answer to you: no names, no accounts, no IP addresses. If a game ever needs to store more than that, this page will say so first.

Spam protection

To stop spam, the server briefly keeps your IP address in memory to limit how often you can submit. It is never written to the database.

Analytics

Page views are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across sites.

Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver pages and block abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Sharing

Share buttons copy your result to the clipboard or opens your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent to the site.
